# Keluaran 12 pola, n=4

## Segitiga siku-siku

`bash scripts/kotlin.sh triangle 4`

```text
*
**
***
****
```

## Segitiga terbalik

`bash scripts/kotlin.sh inverted-triangle 4`

```text
****
***
**
*
```

## Piramida

`bash scripts/kotlin.sh pyramid 4`

```text
   *
  ***
 *****
*******
```

## Piramida terbalik

`bash scripts/kotlin.sh inverted-pyramid 4`

```text

  ***
   *
```
